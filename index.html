FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Lemniscate integrate with?+
Lemniscate runs on the tools you already use — GoHighLevel for CRM and messaging, Meta and Google for ads, Twilio for phone/SMS, plus OpenAI and HeyGen for the AI layer. If you don't have a stack yet, we set one up for you.
How long does setup take?+
Most teams are live within a few days. We connect your tools, train Voss and Nova on your brand and market, and hand you an approve-and-publish workflow — no technical work on your end.
Am I locked into a long contract?+
No long-term lock-in. It's a membership — you stay because it works. We'll cover the exact terms on your demo.
Who owns the data and content?+
You do. Your leads, your CRM records, and everything the Content Factory produces belong to you and stay in your accounts.
Does the AI post without my approval?+
Only if you want it to. By default, everything the system drafts — posts, videos, campaigns — waits for your approval before it goes out.
Is there support if I get stuck?+
Yes. Every plan includes support, and Enterprise members get dedicated onboarding. Nova can also answer product questions right on this site.
